## Silent killers that fire

- **Single-threaded:** Niamh is the only evidenced active buyer contact. Invited colleagues do not count as established relationships until conversations occur.
- **Untested champion:** Bringing colleagues to a demo discussion is helpful, but no evidence shows Niamh taking internal risk to secure budget or a paid decision. She explicitly cannot approve the subscription.
- **Phantom economic buyer:** Leo is named, but has not attended a call; funding and approval are being relayed through Niamh.
- **Seller-generated urgency:** Both the trial schedule and 30 October close date come from the seller. No buyer deadline or dated grant decision supports them.
- **Unquantified pain:** No buyer number or business case supports spending EUR 12,000.
- **Paper process not started:** The seller's close date is 17 days away. There is no evidence that legal or security has received documents, and data access is unagreed. The date is not credible.
- **Happy ears:** “Niamh likes the demo” is sentiment. “We would try it if it is free” supports free evaluation interest, not willingness to buy. The colleague invitation is genuine engagement but cannot carry a paid forecast.
- **Stage inflation:** Proposal implies progress toward a commercial decision, while the only proposal is an unaccepted free trial with no agreed scope or paid buying path. Move to an early qualification stage, or the closest equivalent in this CRM.

## Forecast and realistic timing

**OMIT:** The opportunity scores 2/16 and has neither direct economic-buyer contact nor a mapped paper process within 30 days of the seller's close date.

**There is no defensible paid close date today.** The rep date is 30 October. The proposed trial ends 12 November, and there is no agreed early purchase decision that explains closing before then. Even 12 November is not a buyer-backed milestone because Niamh has not accepted the trial dates.

If the buyer confirms the proposed trial and makes a purchase decision after its completion, planning must account for an additional assumed 3–8 weeks of paperwork unless those steps are confirmed and completed in parallel. If paperwork began only on 12 November, that planning assumption would put signature around **3 December 2026 to 7 January 2027**, conditional on funding and approval. This is an illustrative scenario, not a replacement forecast. The unknown grant date could delay or prevent a purchase entirely.

Keep the opportunity outside the forecast until Leo establishes a credible paid route and the buyer confirms the decision and paper process. If funding remains dependent on an undated grant, retain the relationship as nurture and agree a funding-triggered follow-up.

## The next action

**Highest-leverage gap: a verified paid buying path.** Before designing a free project, determine whether the actual approver can and would buy at EUR 12,000 if an evaluation succeeds.

**Specific action:** Use the 14 October discussion to get Niamh to bring Leo into a commercial qualification conversation and secure written agreement on paid funding, trial scope, success criteria, an end date, and a paid decision meeting before starting any free trial.

The proposed 15 October start is not a commitment. Reschedule it if the terms cannot be agreed beforehand. A workable trial needs one defined inbox, a named operator, agreed data access and review requirements, a measurable buyer-owned outcome, a fixed finish, and a meeting with the approver to decide whether to purchase. Define the support you will provide within that scope; any extension or additional work should require a separate agreement. Do not begin connecting customer data until access and review requirements are agreed.

If Leo cannot establish a credible funding route and decision milestone, pause the free trial and agree to revisit when the funding position changes. That preserves momentum without underwriting an indefinite free project.
