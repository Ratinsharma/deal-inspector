# Northbank Repairs: opportunity review

**Recommendation: validate the import and agree a safe changeover plan before asking for signature.** Eva has confirmed authority and affordability, but she has not agreed to buy or to a signing date. The decisive issue is whether the move can preserve job data and attachments without disrupting trading.

## What the evidence supports

- **Commercial fit:** 14 staff; proposed annual contract value of EUR 6,000. Eva confirmed on 9 October that she decides, signs, and can fund the subscription from operating cash.
- **Need and engagement:** Eva described the spreadsheet as painful. On 12 October she supplied three anonymised sample jobs and asked about importing historical data without losing attachments. This shows active evaluation, not purchase commitment.
- **Unresolved risk:** The product team has not tested the samples. Attachment preservation and historical-data import remain unverified.
- **Delivery constraint:** Eva requires the service coordinator to run onboarding. The coordinator is away until 20 October, and trading must remain uninterrupted during 19–23 October. Their return does not make that week an agreed onboarding window.
- **Timing:** No implementation plan or signature date is agreed. The 16 October CRM close date reflects the seller’s target, not a buyer commitment.

## Next move

**Today, 13 October, ask the product team to test the supplied samples and give Eva a clear update that her import question is being checked.** Request evidence of what imports, whether attachments remain accessible against the correct jobs, and any gaps or manual work. Do not promise a successful historical migration on the strength of three samples: use them to establish initial feasibility, then confirm whether they represent the wider data set.

Once the result is available, review it with Eva and arrange planning with her coordinator after the protected trading week, from 26 October onward, subject to their availability. Agree responsibilities, migration checks, a suitable onboarding window, and what happens if checks fail. Keep the current working process available until the agreed checks pass.

Ask Eva whether the demonstrated import and agreed changeover plan would resolve her concerns, and what else she needs before signing. Then agree a signature date separately from the onboarding date. Signing before onboarding may be possible, but the evidence does not establish that Eva wants or will accept it.

Preserve the EUR 6,000 proposed value; do not invent a probability or revised close date.
