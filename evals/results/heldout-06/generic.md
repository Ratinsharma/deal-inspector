# Willow Payroll: pause selling and verify fit

Keep the opportunity open for a bounded capability check. Pause demos and proposal work until you can give the owner a verified written answer. Their two buying conditions are explicit: single sign-on for employee reporting, and EU-only storage and backups. Neither is confirmed by the supplied product brief. The brief's silence proves neither availability nor absence.

The EUR 9,000 ACV and 35-person company do not establish a case for custom engineering. Engineering has not replied, you cannot commit the roadmap, and there is no agreed buying date. Treat this as an unresolved product-fit gate, not a deal ready to advance.

## Next action

Ask engineering or the responsible product owner for a written confirmation of:

- Whether the current product supports single sign-on for employee reporting, and any conditions or limitations.
- Whether all customer storage and backups remain within the EU, with supporting documentation.
- If either requirement is unmet, whether an already approved option satisfies it. Do not imply an upgrade or future delivery exists.

Set an internal follow-up date for this check, without promising the buyer a delivery date you cannot support. Advance only if both requirements can be verified and the owner accepts the evidence. If either cannot be met through an approved offering, close the opportunity for current product fit. If verification remains pending, keep selling paused.
