## Recorded output

**Recommendation: Advance. Product fit: Supported.** Elm Consulting has tested calendar imports successfully, needs supported reminders, and has a credible approval and implementation path. Liam's renewal-clause check is the remaining purchase condition, not an established blocker.

**Evidence and main blocker**
- **Recorded CRM:** Scheduling subscription; €9,000 ACV; Proposal; rep Ava; owner Liam; close 2026-10-16. Review date: 2026-10-13.
- **Direct buyer email, 2026-10-12:** Liam explicitly owns the firm and can approve this subscription without another sign-off. He requested the standard annual agreement and offered to sign on Friday after reviewing the renewal clause. This supports authority and purchase intent.
- **Product brief and buyer email:** CSV imports and reminders are supported; Liam reports successful trial imports and six hours of manual reminders weekly. Six hours is the reported workload, not established recoverable time or payroll savings.
- **Seller call summary, 2026-10-12:** Liam and Maya will configure reminders; Maya confirmed two hours next week. That supports implementation capacity, although the material does not establish the setup effort required. Liam prefers the tool to their spreadsheet.

**Next action:** Suggested owner Ava, today: send the requested standard agreement and supported setup guide. Proposed wording: “Here is the annual agreement. Does the renewal clause work for you, and are you still comfortable signing on 16 October? Will the planned setup time get reminders live before November?”

**Progress signal:** Liam accepts the renewal terms and confirms signing; Liam or Maya confirms a feasible setup plan. If the clause needs changes, identify the exact concern and the approved resolution before reconfirming timing.

**Forecast note:** Risk-only readout because category definitions are absent. The recorded 16 October close is supported by Liam's dated signing statement, conditional on the clause check. Do not assign a forecast category from this material.
